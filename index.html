1. Executive summary

Verdict: not ready to ship. The dashj → Kotlin SDK cutover itself works: on every path we tried (fresh wallet, seed restore, in-place upgrade from 11.9.0, mainnet and testnet, 2 GB to 3 GB devices, kill/network/memory torture) the cutover committed on the first launch, exactly one SDK engine ran, dashj never started post-cutover, the one-time explainer showed once, the replay finished (4–10 min for the reference wallet, 42 s for an empty mainnet wallet, 3 m 12 s for a 3,000-tx wallet) and the final balance matched the chain to the duff. No LMK, OOM, ANR or app crash was observed in roughly 40 hours of emulator time across 12 streams. But around that core there are 14 S1 defects, of which 9 were reproduced on a device, that lose or hide money, strand funds, bypass the lock screen, or wedge sync with no way out except a force-stop.

What was tested. Twelve device streams and one static-review stream ran in parallel on eight Android 15 emulators (arm64, 2–3 GB RAM) against _testNet3Release builds of master (11.9.0, versionCode 11090002) and fix/upgrade-memory-and-sync @ 616ac58ff (12.0.0, versionCode 12000000), plus prodRelease (mainnet) builds of both. 118 user stories were exercised (matrix in section 2), producing 1,900+ screenshots and 67 videos, all linked from the matrix and the register. The unit suite (2,077 tests) passes. A 3,000-transaction, 800-address synthetic wallet was grown on testnet for the large-wallet stories.

Release gates that are not product bugs but will block or embarrass a release: targetSdk/compileSdk regressed 36 → 35 in every module (SR-23; Play requires 36 since 2026-08-31); the branch has not merged master since 2026-08-06 and is missing 11 commits including fix: repair v11.9 crashes (#1524) and the Android 16 support commit whose lock-screen change D-011 regresses (D-016); the mixed-funds migration is hard-suppressed (D-063).

S2 defects (16) cluster into: balance display during replay (D-002, D-079, D-083), receive-address reuse on SDK keychains (D-003), memory (the SDK replay uses 3–5× the PSS and 10× the native heap of dashj: 600–650 MB PSS on the reference wallet, 995 MB on a 4-tx mainnet wallet; D-025, D-035), offline state never surfaced (D-070), DashPay contact-coverage debt after restore (D-052), history loss on small wallets (D-082), and the static SR-09..SR-27 set (idle stopSelf latch, shielded watchdog bricking, Coinbase fee estimate on dashj, memo dropped on SDK sends, wipe marks complete on failure, restore spinner hang, autosave/size-guard gaps, CoinJoin privacyMode dropped).

S3/S4 (about 80) are individually listed in the register; the recurring themes are fee/amount disclosure (flat 0.0001 fee shown, shielded platform cost of 0.0021 per shield disclosed nowhere, Max always over-fills then silently retries smaller), tx-detail correctness on the SDK seam (change address shown as recipient, duplicated address rows, unshield shown as a receive, missing fee rows, wrong tax-category defaults), CSV export gaps, the SETTLED state never reached, the misleading once-a-minute "idling detected" log, localisation (the whole shielded UI is untranslated; ar-EG mirrors the PIN and amount keypads; he-IL is 89 % English), accessibility (font scale 2.0 clips the send "Continue" button and truncates balances), and the buy-credits flow (the 60 s auto-lock silently cancels a top-up; a DAPI failure broadcasts the asset lock and tells the user it "may have gone through").

What passed cleanly and is worth keeping as regression evidence: the cutover state machine and explainer on every upgrade path; 0 blocks lost across 5 kills during replay; network flaps and low-memory trims during replay; 30-min background idle with zero wakelock growth; instant receive; the shield/unshield/username/invite/contact flows end to end across two wallets; money accounting to the duff under HOME/kill/airplane interruption and triple-tap confirm; gap-limit discovery to index 399 on both chains; wallet-file corruption recovery from the key backup; onboarding offline; the dashj diagnostic toggle with sdk=dashj parity.

Not covered / caveats. File backup/restore and "Restore from file" are DEBUG-only and absent from release; Coinbase/Uphold/Topper stop at the credential step (no keys); the 6-hour foreground-service notice was code-checked only; a true 512 MB-heap / 1.5 GB-RAM device could not be emulated (emulator clamps at 2.5 GB), so LMK behaviour on low-end phones is inferred from footprint, not observed; the filter-stall watchdog never fired in ~40 replays, so SR-01 remains a static finding; testnet CoinJoin never completes a mixing round today (denominations are created, fully-mixed coins are not obtainable), so D-068 was reproduced with denominated coins in the m/9' account; the reference wallet's expected balance in the brief (107.43173749) is stale, the on-chain value is 107.08173522 (a 0.35 send on 2026-09-05).

Suggested fix order. D-037 and D-041 (history/balance truth on the seam) → D-068 + D-063 (stranded CoinJoin funds; decide on the migration) → D-011 + merge master (security regression, SR-23 targetSdk) → D-056 + D-031 + SR-09/SR-06 (engine lifecycle: restart after bind, after idle teardown, and background alarm) → SR-22 + SR-05 (destructive defaults) → SR-01 (watchdog self-cancel) → receive-address pinning (D-003/SR-03) → fee/amount disclosure cluster → localisation and accessibility.
