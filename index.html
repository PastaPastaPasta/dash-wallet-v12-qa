1. Executive summary

Verdict: not ready to ship. The dashj → Kotlin SDK cutover itself works: on every path we tried (fresh wallet, seed restore, in-place upgrade from 11.9.0, mainnet and testnet, 2 GB to 3 GB devices, kill/network/memory torture) the cutover committed on the first launch, exactly one SDK engine ran, dashj never started post-cutover, the one-time explainer showed once, the replay finished (4–10 min for the reference wallet, 42 s for an empty mainnet wallet, 3 m 12 s for a 3,000-tx wallet) and the final balance matched the chain to the duff. No LMK, OOM, ANR or app crash was observed in roughly 40 hours of emulator time across 12 streams. But around that core there are 15 S1 defects, 14 of them reproduced on a device (a second pass of six device streams, SRA–SRF, took the static review's findings to emulators; only SR-02 remains static-only), that lose or hide money, strand funds, bypass the lock screen, wipe or fail to wipe wallets, or wedge sync with no way out except a force-stop.

What was tested. Twelve device streams and one static-review stream ran in parallel on eight Android 15 emulators, followed by six device-reproduction streams (SRA–SRF, seven emulators incl. one Android 12 / API 31) that took every static-review finding to a device: (arm64, 2–3 GB RAM) against _testNet3Release builds of master (11.9.0, versionCode 11090002) and fix/upgrade-memory-and-sync @ 616ac58ff (12.0.0, versionCode 12000000), plus prodRelease (mainnet) builds of both. 118 user stories plus 41 static-review reproductions were exercised (matrix in section 2), producing 2,500+ screenshots and 105 videos, all linked from the matrix and the register. Of the 44 static findings, 28 reproduced on a device (several worse than written), 9 reproduced partially, 2 did not reproduce (SR-02 invite key loss, SR-14 forever-Proving), 2 were blocked by missing integration keys (SR-04, SR-18) and 3 were not attempted (SR-24, SR-37, SR-44). The unit suite (2,077 tests) passes. A 3,000-transaction, 800-address synthetic wallet was grown on testnet for the large-wallet stories.

Release gates that are not product bugs but will block or embarrass a release: targetSdk/compileSdk regressed 36 → 35 in every module (SR-23, confirmed at artefact level; Play requires 36 since 2026-08-31); minSdk regressed 24 → 29 and armeabi-v7a/x86 were dropped, so Android 7–9 and 32-bit ARM users silently stop receiving updates (D-098, SR-39); the mainnet build seeds the SDK flags with no opt-out (SR-08, D-099); the branch has not merged master since 2026-08-06 and is missing 11 commits including fix: repair v11.9 crashes (#1524) and the Android 16 support commit whose lock-screen change D-011 regresses (D-016); the mixed-funds migration is hard-suppressed (D-063).

S2 defects (40) cluster into: balance display during replay (D-002, D-079, D-083), receive-address reuse on SDK keychains (D-003), memory (the SDK replay uses 3–5× the PSS and 10× the native heap of dashj: 600–650 MB PSS on the reference wallet, 995 MB on a 4-tx mainnet wallet; D-025, D-035), offline state never surfaced (D-070), DashPay contact-coverage debt after restore (D-052), history loss on small wallets (D-082), and the SR set, now mostly device-confirmed: the idle stopSelf latch kills the service within 10 s of leaving the home screen and the background alarm re-arms for +24 h with an 18 h window (SR-09, SR-06 downgraded to S2 because it did fire once on an awake, battery-exempt emulator); memo, exchange rate AND amount dropped on every SDK-routed send (SR-19, D-111); the send amount silently rewritten near the balance (D-112); Reset Wallet marks itself complete after 13 swallowed EACCES failures and leaks the old cutover state into the next wallet (SR-20), or silently does nothing at all when the marker cannot be written (SR-36 / D-107); aside wallet copies never reclaimed (SR-26); a shielded send whose ViewModel is cleared still broadcasts while the screen returns re-submittable (SR-13); the shielded stall watchdog locks the transfer screen until the op ends (SR-12); the username fee writes no history row (SR-11); CoinJoin mode orphaned on upgrade (SR-27); Topper crashes the app on tap (D-116); Confirm + PIN on the shielded transfer screen can be a silent no-op (D-120); shielding everything hides the Invitations entry point (D-121); the filter-stall EXHAUSTED path is dead code (SR-35).

S3/S4 (about 80) are individually listed in the register; the recurring themes are fee/amount disclosure (flat 0.0001 fee shown, shielded platform cost of 0.0021 per shield disclosed nowhere, Max always over-fills then silently retries smaller), tx-detail correctness on the SDK seam (change address shown as recipient, duplicated address rows, unshield shown as a receive, missing fee rows, wrong tax-category defaults), CSV export gaps, the SETTLED state never reached, the misleading once-a-minute "idling detected" log, localisation (the whole shielded UI is untranslated; ar-EG mirrors the PIN and amount keypads; he-IL is 89 % English), accessibility (font scale 2.0 clips the send "Continue" button and truncates balances), and the buy-credits flow (the 60 s auto-lock silently cancels a top-up; a DAPI failure broadcasts the asset lock and tells the user it "may have gone through").

What passed cleanly and is worth keeping as regression evidence: the cutover state machine and explainer on every upgrade path; 0 blocks lost across 5 kills during replay; network flaps and low-memory trims during replay; 30-min background idle with zero wakelock growth; instant receive; the shield/unshield/username/invite/contact flows end to end across two wallets; money accounting to the duff under HOME/kill/airplane interruption and triple-tap confirm; gap-limit discovery to index 399 on both chains; wallet-file corruption recovery from the key backup; onboarding offline; the dashj diagnostic toggle with sdk=dashj parity.

Not covered / caveats. File backup/restore and "Restore from file" are DEBUG-only and absent from release; Coinbase/Uphold/Topper stop at the credential step (no keys); the 6-hour foreground-service notice was code-checked only; a true 512 MB-heap / 1.5 GB-RAM device could not be emulated (emulator clamps at 2.5 GB), so LMK behaviour on low-end phones is inferred from footprint, not observed; the filter-stall watchdog was forced to fire in the reproduction pass (SRE) by a per-uid iptables drop, so SR-01 is now device-confirmed; testnet CoinJoin never completes a mixing round today (denominations are created, fully-mixed coins are not obtainable), so D-068 was reproduced with denominated coins in the m/9' account; the reference wallet's expected balance in the brief (107.43173749) is stale, the on-chain value is 107.08173522 (a 0.35 send on 2026-09-05).
