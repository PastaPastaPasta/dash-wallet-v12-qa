Any tx with no txos row is invisible to both writer and checker: transactions = 2190, txos-union = 2079, the 111 missing display rows are exactly the txids absent from the union; the funding tx has zero txos rows. Nothing repairs it: 5-min wait, bg/fg, force-stop+relaunch all leave delta = 111. Balance stays correct; history does not.
