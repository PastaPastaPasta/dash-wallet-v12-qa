                              = sendAllFeeReserveDuffs(1) = (10 + 1*148 + 68) * 3/2

  required - available        = 98,999,819 - 98,999,774 =    45 duffs shortfall

Because the reserve is modelled at 1.5 duffs/byte and the drain is built at 2 duffs/byte, the
floor attempt is short by 25% of the fee on EVERY send-max, regardless of size. The send only
succeeds because SdkL1SendService.kt:1360-1374 catches the throwable, STRING-MATCHES it via
isSendAllShortfall() (SdkL1SendService.kt:342-348 -- type check plus
`message.startsWith("transaction build failed") && message.contains("Insufficient funds")` on the
legacy arm) and retries with floor=1. Any change to the SDK's error text or type silently turns
every send-max into a hard failure.
